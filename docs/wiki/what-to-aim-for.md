# What to aim for

You can reach your first gate in two ways: find one the world built, or build one yourself. [Getting started](getting-started.md) walks through the build block by block. This page covers which way to go and what to work toward after the first gate stands.

## Path A: find a gate

About one structure in four comes with a gate, and each gate has a DHD holding 3 naquadah. Look in:

- Desert pyramids, jungle temples and swamp huts
- Igloos, where the gate is a Pegasus gate
- Ocean monuments and Nether fortresses
- Plains, desert, savanna, snowy and taiga villages

A Tok'ra villager stands guard at most of these gates. The Tok'ra trade the crystals a gate is built from, so a visit pays off even if you plan to build your own gate. One generated gate in seven or so also has a chest beside it holding a ZPM. The full list is in [World generation](worldgen.md).

A found gate works as it is. Put naquadah in its DHD and dial. To dial, though, you need the address of a second gate. Right-click a gate to read its address, so you can find a second generated gate and note its address, or build the second gate yourself.

## Path B: build a gate

1. Mine naquadah. Look below sea level, where ore forms under lava, and bring an iron pickaxe. One gate takes 40 naquadah, plus a few more for DHD fuel.
2. Craft 40 naquadah alloy ingots. Each takes one naquadah and one iron ingot.
3. Collect 40 chiseled sandstone, 40 smooth sandstone, redstone, ender pearls, glowstone dust and an eye of ender.
4. Get a core crystal for the base and a controller crystal for the DHD. Craft them from diamonds, or trade for them with a Tok'ra.
5. Build the ring, then place a DHD and fuel it, as steps 4 to 6 of [Getting started](getting-started.md) describe.

## First goals once a gate stands

A wormhole needs two ends, so make a second gate first. Build it at least 128 blocks from the first, or use a generated one. Then dial its address and walk into the event horizon. Stand clear of the front of the gate while the wormhole opens, because the burst kills anything there.

Keep a record of the addresses you find. Read each one from the gate's screen and add it to your address book with `/sgcraft book add <name> <address>`, or to a PDD's book. [Addresses](addresses.md#address-books) covers both.

## Late game

- Power your gates with a ZPM, which holds a huge store of gate energy. See [ZPM](items/zpm.md).
- Build a [city shield generator](blocks/city-shield-generator.md). Its dome keeps out whatever comes from outside.
- Set [access](access.md) rights on your gates to choose who may dial them, and address rules to choose which gates may connect.

## If you get stuck

If the gate does not form, check that every block is in place and that the nine blocks inside are air. See [Building gates](building-gates.md#forming-a-gate). If a dial fails, the reason shows when you dial. See [Dialing](dialing.md#what-can-stop-a-dial). For anything else, try the [FAQ](faq.md).
