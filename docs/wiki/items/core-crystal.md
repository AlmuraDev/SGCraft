# Stargate Core Crystal

The Stargate Core Crystal is a crafting part for gate bases, gate upgrades, tools and the power unit. You craft it from a diamond, trade for it with a Tok'ra, or find it in chests.

```infobox
Stargate Core Crystal
ID: sgcraft:core_crystal
Category: Materials
Type: Item
Stackable: Yes (64)
Trade: 8 emeralds and 1 diamond (Tok'ra)
```

## Obtaining

### Crafting

Craft it from 1 {{item:minecraft:diamond}}, 5 {{item:minecraft:blue_dye}} and 3 {{item:minecraft:redstone}}:

```recipe
sgcraft:core_crystal
```

### Tok'ra trade

A Tok'ra, the villager who keeps watch at a generated gate, sells it:

| Wants | Gives | Uses | Experience |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 8 {{item:minecraft:emerald}} and 1 {{item:minecraft:diamond}} | 1 Stargate Core Crystal | 12 | 5 |

A data pack can change the trade through the trade set `sgcraft:tokra/level_1`. See [World generation](../worldgen.md#tokra-villagers).

### Chest loot

SGCraft's loot rolls alongside the vanilla loot in these chests:

| Chest | Chance | Count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dungeon | 20% that the pool rolls, 1 to 2 rolls; each roll is a core crystal 2 times in 7 | 1 |
| Bonus chest | Always, 1 to 2 rolls; each roll is a core crystal 1 time in 2 | 1 to 2 |
| Stronghold library | 75% that the pool rolls, 1 to 2 rolls; each roll is a core crystal 1 time in 3 | 1 |
| Village weaponsmith and toolsmith | 50% that the pool rolls, 1 to 2 rolls; each roll is a core crystal 1 time in 4 | 1 |

See [World generation](../worldgen.md#chest-loot).

## Usage

The crystal is a crafting part. It goes into the {{item:sgcraft:stargate_base}}, the {{item:sgcraft:pegasus_upgrade}} and the {{item:sgcraft:universe_upgrade}}, among other recipes.
