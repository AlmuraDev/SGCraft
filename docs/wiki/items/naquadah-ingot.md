# Naquadah Alloy Ingot

The Naquadah Alloy Ingot is the metal most SGCraft recipes use, made from naquadah and iron.

```infobox
Naquadah Alloy Ingot
ID: sgcraft:naquadah_ingot
Category: Materials
Type: Item
Stackable: Yes (64)
```

## Obtaining

### Crafting

Craft one {{item:sgcraft:naquadah}} with one {{item:minecraft:iron_ingot}}:

```recipe
sgcraft:naquadah_ingot
```

Break a {{item:sgcraft:naquadah_block}} back into 9 ingots:

```recipe
sgcraft:naquadah_ingot_from_block
```

### Chest loot

SGCraft's loot rolls alongside the vanilla loot in these chests:

| Chest | Chance | Count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bonus chest | 50% | 32 to 64 |
| Village weaponsmith and toolsmith | 50% that the pool rolls, 1 to 2 rolls; each roll is ingots 2 times in 4 | 1 to 8 |

See [World generation](../worldgen.md#chest-loot).

## Usage

The ingot is a crafting part. Nine ingots make a {{item:sgcraft:naquadah_block}}, which stores them and goes into the {{item:sgcraft:supergate_ring}} and {{item:sgcraft:supergate_base}} recipes.
