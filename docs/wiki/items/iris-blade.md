# Iris Blade

The Iris Blade is one blade of a gate's metal iris. Eight blades and a redstone make a Stargate Iris Upgrade.

```infobox
Iris Blade
ID: sgcraft:iris_blade
Category: Materials
Type: Item
Stackable: Yes (64)
```

## Obtaining

### Crafting

Craft it from 4 {{item:minecraft:iron_ingot}} and 1 {{item:minecraft:charcoal}}:

```recipe
sgcraft:iris_blade
```

The blade isn't found as loot or sold by villagers.

## Usage

Put 8 blades around 1 {{item:minecraft:redstone}} to craft a {{item:sgcraft:iris_upgrade}}, then use the upgrade on a gate to fit its iris. See [The iris](../iris.md#fitting-an-iris).
