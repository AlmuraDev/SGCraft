# Stargate Controller Crystal

The Stargate Controller Crystal is a crafting part for DHDs, the handheld tools and the MALP's console and tablet. You craft it from a diamond, trade for it with a Tok'ra, or find it in chests.

```infobox
Stargate Controller Crystal
ID: sgcraft:controller_crystal
Category: Materials
Type: Item
Stackable: Yes (64)
Trade: 16 emeralds and 1 diamond (Tok'ra)
```

## Obtaining

### Crafting

Craft it from 1 {{item:minecraft:diamond}}, 5 {{item:minecraft:orange_dye}} and 3 {{item:minecraft:redstone}}:

```recipe
sgcraft:controller_crystal
```

### Tok'ra trade

A Tok'ra, the villager who keeps watch at a generated gate, sells it:

| Wants | Gives | Uses | Experience |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 16 {{item:minecraft:emerald}} and 1 {{item:minecraft:diamond}} | 1 Stargate Controller Crystal | 12 | 5 |

A data pack can change the trade through the trade set `sgcraft:tokra/level_1`. See [World generation](../worldgen.md#tokra-villagers).

### Chest loot

SGCraft's loot rolls alongside the vanilla loot in these chests:

| Chest | Chance | Count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dungeon | 20% that the pool rolls, 1 to 2 rolls; each roll is a controller crystal 1 time in 7 | 1 |
| Bonus chest | Always, 1 to 2 rolls; each roll is a controller crystal 1 time in 2 | 1 to 2 |
| Stronghold library | 75% that the pool rolls, 1 to 2 rolls; each roll is a controller crystal 2 times in 3 | 1 |
| Village weaponsmith and toolsmith | 50% that the pool rolls, 1 to 2 rolls; each roll is a controller crystal 1 time in 4 | 1 |

See [World generation](../worldgen.md#chest-loot).

## Usage

The crystal is a crafting part. It goes into the {{item:sgcraft:dhd}}, the {{item:sgcraft:pdd}}, the {{item:sgcraft:gate_control_tablet}}, the {{item:sgcraft:gdo}}, the {{item:sgcraft:configurator}}, the {{item:sgcraft:malp_console}} and the {{item:sgcraft:malp_tablet}}.
