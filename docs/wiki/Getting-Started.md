# Getting Started

1. **Find naquadah.** Naquadah Ore generates in the Overworld, mostly just below lava pools deep underground and in
   small clusters between y 16 and 31. It needs a diamond pickaxe and drops 2 to 6 Naquadah.
2. **Make Naquadah Alloy.** An Iron Ingot and a Naquadah, shapeless, make a Naquadah Alloy Ingot. Almost everything
   in the mod uses it.
3. **Get the crystals.** The Stargate Core Crystal and Stargate Controller Crystal are found in dungeon, stronghold
   library, bonus and village blacksmith chests, or bought from a Tok'ra villager (8 emeralds and a diamond for a Core
   Crystal, 16 and a diamond for a Controller Crystal). Their crafting recipes are off unless the server turns them on.
4. **Craft the gate:** 8 Stargate Ring Blocks, 7 Stargate Chevron Blocks and a Stargate Base (one Core Crystal). See
   [Recipes](Recipes).
5. **Build it.** Place the base, then the ring around it in the pattern on [Building Stargates](Building-Stargates).
   When the last block goes in, the gate merges into one ring.
6. **Add a DHD.** Craft a Stargate Controller (one Controller Crystal) and place it within about 5 blocks of the
   gate, facing it. It links on its own.
7. **Fuel it.** Click the lower half of the DHD and put Naquadah in its fuel slots. Each piece pays for 24 openings
   of a nearby gate by default; far or other-world gates cost more (see [Power](Power)).
8. **Dial.** Click the top of the DHD, type the other gate's address (shown when you right-click that gate's base),
   and press the centre dome or Enter.

From there, see [Addresses and Dialing](Addresses-and-Dialing) for the hand dialers and the
[Configurator](Configurator) for the gate's settings.
