# Transport Rings and Obelisks

## Transport Rings

A pair of linked ring platforms swaps everything standing in one set of rings with everything in the other.

**Blocks:** Transport Ring Platform (Floor), Transport Ring Platform (Ceiling), Ring Control Panel (a wall panel),
and the Ring Remote (handheld).

### Setting up

1. Place two platforms. Whoever places a platform owns it.
2. **Sneak-right-click** one, then the other, to link them. Sneak-clicking the same platform again cancels. Both must
   be in the same world, within 2,000 blocks of each other (server setting), idle, and you need the right to configure
   both. Linking again replaces the old link.
3. A Ceiling platform rings down to a floor up to 12 blocks below it.

### Using them

- A **Ring Control Panel** starts the nearest platform within 16 blocks.
- A **Ring Remote** starts the platform you're standing in, or else the nearest one within 8 blocks.
- The rings rise at both ends, the screen fades to white and black, and everything in the rings (3 x 3 blocks, about
  2.6 high) swaps places. Players are held still and can't be hurt from the moment the rings start until they're
  back down.

### Power

The sending platform pays, in FE: 20,000 per use plus 20 per block of distance (server settings). It stores up to
200,000 FE, takes FE from cables on any side, and burns Naquadah from its 4 fuel slots (right-click the platform)
when it runs short, at 100,000 FE a piece.

### Access

The owner can always use and configure a platform. Right-click it with a [Configurator](Configurator) to set who else
may: everyone (the default) except listed players, or only listed players; listed admins may configure it.
Server operators can always use and configure it.

## Asgard Obelisks

Two-block-tall pillars that beam a player to their linked partner, with no distance limit, even in another world, and
no power cost. They come in two styles, the **Asgard Transport Obelisk** (stone) and the **Asgard Knotwork Obelisk**
(sandstone); an obelisk only links with one of its own style.

- **Link:** sneak-right-click one obelisk, then the other (you need the right to configure both). Sneak-click the
  first again to cancel. Linking again replaces the old link.
- **Beam:** stand within 4 blocks and click the gem. After about 2.5 seconds you're beamed to 2 blocks in front of
  the other obelisk's gem. Moving cancels it.
- **Access:** as for ring platforms: owner, Configurator access list, server operators.
