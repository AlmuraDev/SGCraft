# The community

Stargate has had no new television series since 2011, and its fans have kept it going all the same. This page is about the sites, shows and events that hold the fandom together, the Stargate mods Minecraft players have made, and the people behind SGCraft.

### The people who tell the story

GateWorld began on October 22, 1999, the night Showtime aired the SG-1 episode "Jolinar's Memories". Darren Sumner, a fan in Chicago, wanted a site that tracked the show's continuity, and he started it under the name STARGUIDE on his own web space. It moved to SciFiGuide.net within a year and later became GateWorld. More than 25 years later it still breaks news. When Amazon MGM Studios announced Martin Gero's series in November 2025, GateWorld had the story first, and Sumner and David Read talked it through with the producers.

David Read, a former co-editor of GateWorld, hosts Dial the Gate. The show sits down with the people who made Stargate, from the leads to the crew, for interviews that often run several parts. In July 2026 Michael Shanks, who played Daniel Jackson, announced his own SG-1 rewatch podcast with Read as producer. The announcement set the first episode for mid-October 2026 and promised a guest from the show on every episode.

### Conventions

Fans have met in person for more than 25 years. Gatecon, a fan-run convention in Vancouver where the shows were filmed, began in 2000 and has been held on and off since. In the United Kingdom, Starfury ran its Stargate convention, Ascension, in 2025 and set Ascension 2 for August 28 to 30, 2026, at the Hilton Metropole in Birmingham. Stargate actors also appear at general fan events: Amanda Tapping, Michael Shanks and Lexa Doig were announced as guests of Ottawa Comiccon in September 2026.

### Save Stargate

When Amazon MGM Studios dropped Martin Gero's series in June 2026 (see [The Stargate franchise](stargate-history.md#the-franchise-today)), fans organized within days. A petition on Change.org passed 100,000 verified signatures. Fans paid for a banner flown over Amazon MGM's studios in Culver City on June 12, rallied under billboards in Times Square on June 27, and brought the campaign to San Diego Comic-Con in July. GateWorld covered each step. The series has not come back.

## Stargate in Minecraft

### gcewing's SGCraft

SGCraft began as **SG Craft**, a Forge mod by Greg Ewing, known as gcewing. It gave Minecraft gates in the style of SG-1, with a DHD, addresses and a wormhole you could walk through, and it could take power from IndustrialCraft 2 or Thermal Expansion and be run from ComputerCraft or OpenComputers.

The mod's [home page](https://www.cosc.canterbury.ac.nz/greg.ewing/minecraft/mods/SGCraft/) lists releases for Minecraft 1.4.6 through 1.10.2, starting with version 0.2.0, with versions for 1.5, 1.6, 1.7 and 1.8 between. gcewing released it as free software and put the source on GitHub in January 2016. Its [CurseForge page](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/sg-craft) has over 1.5 million downloads. The last release there was 1.13.3 for Minecraft 1.7.10, on January 7, 2017. gcewing also made ArchitectureCraft, a building mod.

### AlmuraDev's SGCraft for 1.12

AlmuraDev (Almura Development Studios) took the mod to Minecraft 1.12 in 2017 and released it on CurseForge as [Stargate Network](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/stargate-network), a continuation of gcewing's SGCraft. Mike Howe, known as Dockter, made most of the changes, with help from other developers. Over the next three years it gained a Pegasus gate, ZPM power, Tok'ra settings for each world and a long list of fixes. The 1.12.2 line reached SGCraft 2.0.5 in August 2020, and it has about 2.9 million downloads. In October 2026 Chris Sanders added the Universe gate and supergates to the 1.12 code, and the remake carries them over.

### SGCraft 3.0

SGCraft 3.0 is a remake for Minecraft 26.3, on NeoForge and Fabric. It is a new codebase that keeps gcewing's gates, timings and rules where it can. It adds a full set of `/sgcraft` commands for server admins, an API for add-ons, and the [MALP](malp.md). The code for every version, from gcewing's first commit to this one, is on [GitHub](https://github.com/AlmuraDev/SGCraft).

## Getting involved

SGCraft is open source. You can help by:

- reporting a bug or asking for a feature on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/AlmuraDev/SGCraft/issues). Say which version of SGCraft, Minecraft and the loader you use, and what you did.
- sending code as a pull request on [GitHub](https://github.com/AlmuraDev/SGCraft). The code is under the MIT license.
- building an add-on on the [API](api.md), for features or links to other mods that belong outside the base mod.
- fixing or adding to this wiki, which lives in the repository's `docs/wiki` folder.
