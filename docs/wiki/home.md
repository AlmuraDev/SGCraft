# SGCraft

SGCraft puts Stargates in Minecraft. You build a ring, dial an address and walk through a wormhole to another gate, in the same world or another one.

SGCraft 3.0 is AlmuraDev's remake of gcewing's SGCraft, rebuilt for NeoForge and Fabric on Minecraft 26.3. It keeps the original's gates, timings and rules where it can. New in this version are a server command suite, an add-on API and the MALP. The gate models are measured from third-party models, which keep their own licenses; the mod's `CREDITS.md` lists them.

If you are coming from SGCraft 2 on Minecraft 1.12, [Changes since SGCraft 2](changes.md) lists what is new, what works differently and what is not ported yet.

## What you can do

- Build Milky Way, Pegasus and Universe gates, as standard 5 by 5 rings or as supergates up to 61 blocks across.
- Dial with a DHD, a handheld dialer, a tablet or a command. Each gate has an address made of glyphs, such as {{glyphs:XOBF-R3@}}.
- Travel between worlds. Mobs, items, minecarts and riders travel too.
- Fit an iris that stops anything coming through, and open it from a distance.
- Power gates with naquadah, or with FE from other mods' cables, which SGCraft turns into gate energy.
- Find gates already built at desert pyramids, jungle temples, ocean monuments, Nether fortresses, swamp huts, igloos and villages.
- Send a MALP through a gate and drive it from a console while you watch its camera.
